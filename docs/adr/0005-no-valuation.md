# The site never gives a Valuation

The site describes past Sales. It does not estimate what a specific home is worth or say whether an asking price is fair. Medians from Built surface and small Quartiers cannot support a judgement about one home, because they know nothing about its condition, floor or view. A figure presented as an estimate could also mislead someone making a major purchase.

This rules out features such as a price checker or an estimate for an address. Those features will be suggested again, so this record exists to show that the limit is deliberate.

## Consequences

- The site must say clearly that it gives no Valuation.
- Small samples must be flagged, so that Visitors do not read too much into them.
